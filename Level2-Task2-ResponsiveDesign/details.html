THE STORY

Knowledge carried through generations.

Heritage lives through people. It can be found in the skills they practise, the materials they use, the stories they remember and the places they call home.

Every archive entry in ROOTS offers a small window into these living traditions. The purpose is not simply to preserve an object or image, but to understand the knowledge and meaning connected to it.

✦ ROOTS / NOTE

A tradition becomes meaningful when it remains connected to the people, places and practices that give it life.

LIVING HERITAGE

More than an object.

Behind every craft, material and inherited object is a network of knowledge. Techniques are learned, adapted and shared through practice.

ROOTS looks beyond the finished object to the hands, memories and communities that make it meaningful.

WHY IT MATTERS

Heritage stays alive when knowledge is shared.

The value of a tradition is not limited to its history. Its knowledge continues to matter when people practise it, teach it and carry it into everyday life.
